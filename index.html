Oakle: the daily Pokémon Yellow challenge

Every day a new random team of five Pokémon lands in your hands, the same team for every player, and you get one try to beat Professor Oak's unused Gen 1 team in the real Pokémon Yellow, running in your browser.

  • Talk to Prof. Oak when you are ready. Before that you can reorder your team with START, then POKEMON.
  • Everyone gets the same team and the same dice each day. One try per day, no rematches.
  • Fewest turns wins the day. Missed a day? Play it from the archive: it still counts towards your wins.
  • Only the first try counts. After that you can play the day again for practice.
  • Some days are brutal on purpose.
